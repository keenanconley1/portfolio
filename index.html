Approach

03 · Working Style

Healthcare communications runs on two clocks at once. A press release moves through legal, medical, and regulatory review before a single word reaches an investor; precision isn't a preference there, it's the job. A LinkedIn post has to move fast enough to matter while still surviving the same scrutiny. I worked both sides at Zai Lab: co-authoring regulated disclosures on clinical data and market access within a cross-functional team, and independently producing executive and social content, including owning MLR routing and coordinating creative with our agency end to end.

The throughline is translation. A Phase 1 response rate and a five-year partnership anniversary are different kinds of news, but both have to land the same way: accurate enough for a regulator, clear enough for an investor, and human enough for anyone else reading.
